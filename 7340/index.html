Maktabah Maftoohah — Sahih al-Bukhari

Sahih al-Bukhari #7340

Kitab Berpegang Teguh Kepada al-Quran dan Sunnah (كتاب الاعتصام بالكتاب والسنة)
حَدَّثَنَا مُسَدَّدٌ، حَدَّثَنَا عَبَّادُ بْنُ عَبَّادٍ، حَدَّثَنَا عَاصِمٌ الأَحْوَلُ، عَنْ أَنَسٍ، قَالَ حَالَفَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم بَيْنَ الأَنْصَارِ وَقُرَيْشٍ فِي دَارِي الَّتِي بِالْمَدِينَةِ‏.‏ وَقَنَتَ شَهْرًا يَدْعُو عَلَى أَحْيَاءٍ مِنْ بَنِي سُلَيْمٍ
Bahasa Melayu: "Narrated Anas:The Prophet (ﷺ) brought the Ansar and the Quraish people into alliance in my house at Medina, and he invoked Allah for one month against the tribe of Bani Sulaim in (the last rak`a of each compulsory) prayer"
English: "Narrated Anas:The Prophet (ﷺ) brought the Ansar and the Quraish people into alliance in my house at Medina, and he invoked Allah for one month against the tribe of Bani Sulaim in (the last rak`a of each compulsory) prayer"
