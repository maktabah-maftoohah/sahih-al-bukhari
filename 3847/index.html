Maktabah Maftoohah — Sahih al-Bukhari

Sahih al-Bukhari #3847

Kitab Keutamaan Kaum Ansar (كتاب مناقب الأنصار) : Hadis 72
وَقَالَ ابْنُ وَهْبٍ أَخْبَرَنَا عَمْرٌو، عَنْ بُكَيْرِ بْنِ الأَشَجِّ، أَنَّ كُرَيْبًا، مَوْلَى ابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ حَدَّثَهُ أَنَّ ابْنَ عَبَّا...

"Narrated Ibn 'Abbas:To run along the valley between two green pillars of Safa and Marwa (mountains) was not Sunna, but the people in the pre-islamic period of ignorance used to run..."
