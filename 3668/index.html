Maktabah Maftoohah — Sahih al-Bukhari

Sahih al-Bukhari #3668

Kitab Keutamaan Para Sahabat Nabi ﷺ (كتاب فضائل أصحاب النبي) : Hadis 19
حَدَّثَنَا إِسْمَاعِيلُ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، حَدَّثَنَا سُلَيْمَانُ بْنُ بِلاَلٍ، عَنْ هِشَامِ بْنِ عُرْوَةَ، عَنْ عُرْوَةَ بْنِ الزُّبَيْرِ...

"Narrated 'Aisha: (the wife of the Prophet) Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) died while Abu Bakr was at a place called As-Sunah (Al-'Aliya) 'Umar stood up and said, "By Allah! Allah's Messenge..."
